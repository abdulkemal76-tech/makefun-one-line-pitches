Entry for the make.fun bounty [A Day of One-Line Pitches](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3): a program that writes one sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

## The sentence

> Just describe your idea to an AI agent — no code, nothing to install — and by tonight you could be tapping the app that texts your mum when your bus is late.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
